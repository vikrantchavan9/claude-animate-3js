# Reel script: "You need 2 hours at the gym" (40 s)

**Fix:** a 15-minute, no-equipment routine.

**Visual direction:** clean flat vector, a navy and white palette with one bright accent colour, a single modern sans-serif typeface, and no cultural or regional styling. Sound design is minimal: a soft tick on each step and a chime on completion.

## Characters

- **ME: Coach Vik.** A calm coach in a plain tracksuit, drawn as a simple geometric figure. He starts the reel convinced that longer sessions are better, so the viewer learns alongside him.
- **SIDEKICK: Tick.** A small stopwatch robot with a face on its dial and hands that double as arms. Deadpan and precise, he counts everything and never wastes a word. His signature is a crisp tick on every step and a chime when a step is complete.

## Script

| # | Speaker | Line | What's on screen |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 (0–1.5 s) | ME | You don't need two hours at the gym! | **Chip:** MYTH: 2 HOURS AT THE GYM. **Card:** timer counting down fast from 2:00:00 to 0:15:00, with a strike through "2:00:00". |
| 2 (1.5–3.5 s) | SIDEKICK | Fifteen minutes. Four moves. Zero equipment. | **Chip:** TICK, THE STOPWATCH. **Card:** numbers counting up: "15 MIN", "4 MOVES", "0 EQUIPMENT". |
| 3 (3.5–6 s) | ME | Tick, prove it. I trained two hours today. | **Chip:** COACH KAI, 2 HOURS TODAY. **Card:** Coach slumped over, with an energy bar nearly empty. |
| 4 (6–8.5 s) | SIDEKICK | Longer isn't better. Consistent is better. | **Chip:** THE RULE. **Card:** big text "Consistent > Long". |
| 5 (8.5–11 s) | ME | Fine. Teach me the fifteen-minute routine. | **Chip:** THE 15-MINUTE ROUTINE. **Card:** empty checklist: ☐ Warm-up 2:00 · ☐ Four moves · ☐ Three rounds 12:00 · ☐ Stretch 1:00 = 15:00. |
| 6 (11–14 s) | SIDEKICK | Step one. Warm up. Two minutes. | **Chip:** STEP 1: WARM-UP. **Card:** checklist item ☑ Warm-up 2:00 ticks off; 2:00 timer counts down. |
| 7 (14–17.5 s) | SIDEKICK | Step two. Squat, pushup, lunge, plank. | **Chip:** STEP 2: FOUR MOVES. **Card:** four icons pop in one by one: squat, pushup, lunge, plank. |
| 8 (17.5–20 s) | ME | Pushups hurt my knees, Tick. | **Chip:** STEP 2: FOUR MOVES. **Card:** pushup icon wobbles; Coach winces. |
| 9 (20–22.5 s) | SIDEKICK | Knees down is fine. Keep going. | **Chip:** KNEES DOWN COUNTS. **Card:** pushup icon switches to the knees-down version with a green ✔. |
| 10 (22.5–26 s) | SIDEKICK | Step three. Forty seconds on, twenty off. | **Chip:** STEP 3: 40 ON / 20 OFF. **Card:** interval ring filling green for 40 s, then grey for 20 s. |
| 11 (26–29 s) | SIDEKICK | Step four. Three rounds. Twelve minutes total. | **Chip:** STEP 4: 3 ROUNDS. **Card:** round dots 1, 2, 3 fill in; total counts up 0:00 to 12:00. |
| 12 (29–31.5 s) | SIDEKICK | Step five. Stretch one minute. Done. | **Chip:** STEP 5: STRETCH. **Card:** checklist fully ticked; total counts up to 15:00 with a stamp "DONE". |
| 13 (31.5–34 s) | ME | That's it? Fifteen minutes, no equipment? | **Chip:** NO EQUIPMENT, ANY ROOM. **Card:** big "15:00" with Coach's jaw dropping. |
| 14 (34–36 s) | SIDEKICK | Every day beats one big day. | **Chip:** CONSISTENCY WINS. **Card:** calendar with seven ticks filling in, one chime per tick. |
| 15 (36–40 s) | ME | Comment Routine and I'll send you the full guide. | **Chip:** COMMENT "ROUTINE". **Card:** the word ROUTINE pulsing large, with "full guide" underneath. |

## Notes

- **Hook (first 3 seconds):** line 1 states the topic and the countdown from 2:00:00 to 0:15:00 is the proof; line 2 is the surprise (a stopwatch robot answers with three hard numbers).
- **Lesson:** all five numbered steps are spoken by the sidekick and add up to 2 + 12 + 1 = 15 minutes (2 min warm-up, 4 moves × 1 min × 3 rounds, 1 min stretch).
- **Safety line for the caption:** General fitness info, not medical advice. Stop if anything hurts, and check with a doctor if you have a health condition.
- **Series idea:** Tick always has the last precise number, and Coach Kai always learns something.
